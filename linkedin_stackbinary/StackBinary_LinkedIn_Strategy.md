# StackBinary — LinkedIn Company Page Strategy & Posting Plan

**Account:** StackBinary company page (founder + employee amplified)
**Primary goal:** Inbound leads / demand generation
**Core audience:** Marketing leaders (CMO/VP/Growth) · Founders & CEOs (SMB–mid-market) · E-commerce / D2C brands
**Core narrative:** *Build. Own. Compound.* — Own your marketing stack. Stop renting it.

---

## 1. Positioning: the one idea every post reinforces

StackBinary sells a **belief shift** before it sells a service:

> "You're renting 10 generic SaaS tools, using 49% of the features, paying forever, and you own none of the IP. There's another way: build one system around *your* workflows, own the data and the code, and let it compound."

Every post should ladder up to one of three "aha" beliefs:

1. **The SaaS math is broken** — license creep, feature bloat, 49% utilization, recurring cost with zero equity.
2. **Build is now faster than buy** — AI-accelerated builds ship in weeks, not quarters; "1 in 4 new martech capabilities are now built, not bought."
3. **Ownership compounds** — your data, your design, your IP; every build makes the next one cheaper.

**Proof assets to reuse constantly:** 55+ brands (Abbott, Sanofi, Philips, KFC, Hyundai, Sony Pictures, Shiseido, Sugar Cosmetics, The Ordinary, Steve Madden), 29+ Shopify stores, 8+ industries / 3 continents, 90-day delivery model, flagship products (AI Ad Intelligence & Ad-Ops, Zyflus, AtoEmail, AI Call Center, TRIBE v2 neural creative analysis).

## 3. Cadence & rhythm

- **Frequency:** 4 posts/week to start (Mon, Tue, Thu + one Fri/weekend "opinion" post). Consistency > volume — do not burst then vanish.
- **Best windows (IST, B2B audience):** Tue–Thu, 9:30–11:00 AM. Test one 7–8 AM slot for founders.
- **Format mix per week:** 1 text+image "hot take," 1 carousel (document post — highest saves/dwell), 1 case-study/proof post, 1 short-form or founder-video/POV.
- **Never** post a bare external link in the body (algorithm penalizes it). Put links in the **first comment** and say "link in comments."

---

## 4. Formats that win on LinkedIn (2026) — ranked for this account

1. **Document / carousel posts** — best for frameworks, "SaaS stack teardown," before/after. High dwell + saves.
2. **Text + single bold image/graphic** — for hot takes and proof stats. Fast to produce.
3. **Founder talking-head video (30–60s)** — high reach; use for POV and product reveals.
4. **Data/visual "proof" graphic** — "49% of martech features go unused" style stat cards.
5. **Poll** — 1x/2 weeks, for demand research + reach ("Would you rather rent or own your stack?").

---

## 5. The amplification engine (non-negotiable for a company page)

Company-page reach is low by design. For every post:

- **Founder reshares within 60 minutes** with a personal one-line take (not a bare reshare).
- **3–6 employees** react + leave a *substantive* comment in the first hour (comments > likes for reach).
- **Reply to every comment** within the first 2 hours — doubles the post's distribution window.
- Seed 2–3 posts/month by **tagging a happy client or partner** (with permission) — borrows their network.
- Repurpose top posts into the **founder's personal profile** natively (not a reshare) a few days later.

---

## 6. Lead capture — turning reach into pipeline

- **Page setup first:** custom CTA button ("Visit website" → martech page), tagline = "Build. Own. Compound. Custom martech you own.", featured products in the About section, a lead-gen form or Calendly link in the pinned featured section.
- **Soft CTAs** (most posts): "Curious what this looks like for your stack? DM me 'OWN'." / "Comment 'STACK' and I'll send the teardown."
- **Hard CTAs** (proof/product posts, ~20%): "We map your current martech spend + build plan in a free 90-min audit → [link in comments]."
- **Lead magnet to build once, promote monthly:** *"The Build-vs-Buy Martech Scorecard"* (PDF) — quantifies license creep and what's cheaper to own. Gated by DM keyword or a simple form → feeds your B2B Lead CRM.
- **Retarget:** run a small paid budget to boost your 2–3 best-performing organic posts to a job-title audience (CMO, Head of Growth, Founder, D2C).

## 8. KPIs — what to actually watch

**Leading (weekly):** impressions, engagement rate (aim >3% early), comments per post, profile/page visits, follower growth, saves on carousels.
**Lead indicators (monthly):** DMs / keyword replies, lead-magnet requests, audit bookings, click-throughs to the martech page.
**Lagging (quarterly):** SQLs sourced from LinkedIn, pipeline value, closed deals attributed.

**Feedback loop:** every 2 weeks, kill the bottom-performing format, clone the top one. Post themes that get *comments* (not just likes) — comments predict pipeline.
