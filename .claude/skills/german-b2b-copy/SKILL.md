# German B2B copy for Stackbinary

Draft natively in German. Never translate an English page sentence by
sentence: translated rhythm is what German buyers detect and distrust.
Write the German page from the same facts, as a German writer would.

## Register

- **Sie-Form, always.** No du, no mixed forms. B2B Germany is Sie.
- Sachlich und präzise. German B2B copy earns trust through specifics,
  not enthusiasm. Cut superlatives the English version tolerates:
  "revolutionär", "einzigartig", "weltklasse" all cost credibility.
  One concrete number beats three adjectives.
- Claims carry evidence in the same sentence: "55+ ausgelieferte
  Produkte", "Antwort in unter 24 Stunden". Unbelegte Behauptungen sind
  in Deutschland Werbesprache, und Werbesprache wird überblättert.
- Anglicisms: keep the established ones (Software, Chatbot, Workflow,
  Cloud, KI-Agent), avoid decorative ones (kein "Journey", kein
  "State-of-the-art", kein "Boost"). When a German term is the search
  term, it wins: Individualsoftware, not Custom Software.

## Terminology (fixed decisions, do not re-debate)

| Concept | Use | Never |
|---|---|---|
| AI | KI | AI (except in product names) |
| custom software | Individualsoftware / individuelle Software | Custom Software |
| company name | Stackbinary | StackBinary |
| voice agent | KI-Telefonassistent / Voicebot | Voice Agent alone |
| customer service | Kundenservice | Customer Care |
| GDPR | DSGVO | GDPR |
| SMB/mid-market | Mittelstand / KMU | SMB |

## Compliance wording (mirrors the English rule)

English rule: "SOC 2-aligned" and "HIPAA-ready", never "certified".
German equivalents: "an SOC 2 orientiert", "HIPAA-ready" (leave
English), and for data protection "DSGVO-konform umgesetzt" only when
describing concrete practices we actually implement (AVV, Hosting-Wahl,
Datenminimierung). Never "zertifiziert", never a blanket
"DSGVO-zertifiziert" (no such certificate exists; Germans know).

## Dashes (owner rule, applies in German too)

No em dashes and no en dashes in customer-facing copy, including the
German Gedankenstrich. Rephrase with commas, colons or parentheses.
Hyphens inside compound words are fine and often mandatory
(KI-Agentur, E-Mail-Marketing).

## Numbers, prices, formats

- German formats: 1.500 € (dot thousands, comma decimals, EUR after
  the number with a space). Dates: 24. August 2026.
- Pricing follows the aiServicesPages rule, adapted: EUR figures appear
  ONLY in FAQ answers, framed as published market rate versus our
  roughly-half figure. Hourly comparisons may appear elsewhere.
- ß and umlauts always correct: heißt, Lösungen, maßgeschneidert.
  Never ae/oe/ue substitutions, never ss where ß belongs.

## SEO specifics for google.de

- The German keyword goes in title, H1 and first paragraph in its
  searched form: if the term is "Individualsoftware entwickeln lassen",
  the page says exactly that, not a synonym.
- Kosten questions get their own H2 plus FAQ entry: Germans search
  "kosten", "was kostet", "preis" far more than English markets. Answer
  with real ranges.
- Meta descriptions in German, under 155 characters, Sie-Form.

## Review loop (non-negotiable)

Every German page, ad or article is reviewed by the German sales
colleague before it ships. Where market idiom is uncertain, leave an
inline HTML comment: <!-- DE-REVIEW: unsicher ob "X" oder "Y" üblich -->
so review is fast and targeted. Nothing German goes live unreviewed.
