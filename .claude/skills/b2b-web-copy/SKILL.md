# Stackbinary B2B web copy

The owner's verdict on essay-style page copy: "seems so wrong and
unprofessional." Service pages follow the professional B2B register of
the SERP incumbents, executed cleaner, not a contrarian essay format.

## Register

- Confident and direct, never smug. The reader is a buyer evaluating
  vendors, not an audience to be entertained.
- Benefit-led sentences: what the client gets, then how we do it.
- Specifics over adjectives: "55+ shipped products", "weekly demo
  builds", "IP assigned on payment". One number beats three claims.
- BANNED tones: snark about competitors ("logo walls", "you should be
  suspicious"), self-deprecation or residency apologia, cleverness for
  its own sake ("a screenshot that compiles"), lecture framings ("What
  X Usually Gets Wrong", "the honest answer is").
- Competitors are never mocked. Differentiate by proof, not by
  describing others' weaknesses.
- Do not volunteer internal structure (where engineering sits, office
  nature, cost base). State offices and capabilities plainly; explain
  pricing by efficiency and delivery model, never by labor arbitrage.

## SEO service-page format (from SERP teardowns 2026-08)

- Exact target keyword in title, H1 and first paragraph, in its
  searched form. City/geo attached where the page targets one.
- H2s carry keyword variants naturally ("Mobile App Development
  Services in Dubai"), one theme per H2.
- Sections in order: hero with proof stats, intro (what we do, who
  for), services grid, industry expertise, why-choose-us, process,
  tech stack, compliance/trust, FAQs, form CTA.
- Cost questions answered in FAQs per the standing pricing rules of
  the target market (currency rules differ per market; check the
  page's data-file header).
- FAQs answer the question in the first sentence, then add detail.

## Standing house rules (apply everywhere, do not re-debate)

- No em or en dashes anywhere in customer-facing copy.
- "Stackbinary", never "StackBinary".
- Compliance: "SOC 2-aligned", "HIPAA-ready", "PDPL-aware",
  "DSGVO-konform umgesetzt" (DE). Never "certified".
- No named products on services pages; systems described generically.
- Never invent clients, numbers, addresses or certifications.
- German copy is governed by german-b2b-copy, not this skill.
