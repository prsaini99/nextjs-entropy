## Read this first: two kinds of pain-point query

Not all pain is winnable for a custom-build shop, and the difference is sharp.

**Type A — pain with a mature SaaS answer.** `customer data platform`,
`whatsapp business api`, `marketing attribution software`. Someone typing these
wants a product they can buy this week. You would be bidding against funded SaaS
companies with better message match, higher budgets and a free trial button. High
CPCs, low win rate. **Deprioritise these.**

**Type B — pain where no clean product fits.** `sync crm with ads`,
`replace multiple marketing tools`, `custom integration development`,
`internal tool development`. The searcher has *already concluded* off-the-shelf
won't work — that's why the query is phrased as a problem, not a product. This is
where a builder wins, because you're not displacing a product, you're answering a
question no product answers. **These are your ground.**

Blocks below are tagged **[A]** or **[B]**. If time is short, run the **[B]**
blocks first: **P3, P6, P7, P12**.

## What each block is testing

**P1 — Reporting & dashboards [A/B]**
`marketing dashboard software` → `agency client dashboard`
Agency reporting is a real, chronic pain. Type A at the tool end, Type B at
"white label" and "multi client" — those are where agencies give up on products.

**P2 — Attribution & tracking [A]**
`marketing attribution software` → `conversion tracking setup`
Expect high CPCs; this is a crowded enterprise SaaS category. But
`ga4 migration services` and `server side tracking setup` are *services* queries —
watch those two specifically.

**P3 — Data integration & silos [B] ← priority**
`customer data platform` → `marketing data silos`
The purest expression of your build-vs-buy pitch. `sync crm with ads`,
`custom integration development` and `zapier alternative` are people who have
already hit the ceiling of off-the-shelf.

**P4 — WhatsApp & India channels [A]**
`whatsapp business api` → `regional language marketing`
Almost certainly the highest-volume block in India. Mostly Type A — BSPs dominate
`whatsapp business api` — but `whatsapp crm integration` is Type B, and it maps
directly onto your existing B2B Lead CRM with WhatsApp capture.

**P5 — Lead response & follow-up [A/B]**
`lead management software` → `missed lead recovery`
Maps to your Lead CRM and AI Call Center. `speed to lead` and `missed lead
recovery` are pain-phrased, not product-phrased — the good kind.

**P6 — Cost reduction & consolidation [B] ← priority**
`reduce saas costs` → `software rationalization`
Literally the `WhyCustom.jsx` argument as a search query. Low volume likely, but
anyone typing `replace multiple marketing tools` is pre-sold on your thesis.

**P7 — AI adoption [B] ← priority**
`ai for marketing teams` → `ai automation for business`
Fast-growing, and `ai agent development company` / `ai automation agency` are
explicitly builder-seeking. Note `mcp integration` already came back 100–1K India
and 1K–10K global at a ₹10 floor — this neighbourhood is worth mapping properly.

**P8 — Personalization & segmentation [A]**
`personalization engine` → `customer journey mapping tool`

**P9 — Content production at scale [A]**
`content automation tool` → `digital asset management marketing`
Related to the Sugar Cosmetics case study (content pipeline, −40% production cost).

**P10 — E-commerce / D2C growth ops [A/B]**
`d2c growth agency` → `marketplace integration software`
`shopify store development` and `shopify plus agency` already showed 100–1K India
and 1K–10K global, so this neighbourhood has proven demand.

**P11 — Compliance & consent [B]**
`consent management platform` → `data privacy consulting india`
DPDP Act 2023 is creating fresh, unserved demand in India. Nobody has a default
answer yet — classic Type B timing.

**P12 — Agency & marketing operations [B] ← priority**
`agency management software` → `revenue operations software`
Agencies are your best-fit buyer: they feel tool sprawl hardest, they resell, and
`Zyflus` already has "multi-brand isolation for agencies" built in.

**P13 — Custom build, general [B]**
`custom dashboard development` → `custom erp development`
Not marketing-specific, but it is the vocabulary of someone who has decided to
build. Included as a control: if these outperform the martech terms, that tells
you something important about how to position the whole site.
