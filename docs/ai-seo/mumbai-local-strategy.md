# Mumbai-first local strategy

Rank as **the** Mumbai tech/martech agency before fighting pan-India terms.
City-level queries have a fraction of the competition, and both classic local
SEO and AI answers lean heavily on location signals we can own quickly.

## Why we can win Mumbai fast

- Real office: Spring Grove Towers, Kandivali East, Mumbai 400101.
- Mumbai-anchored client roster: Mumbai Indians, Reliance Entertainment,
  Sony Pictures, Piramal, Future Group — proof local competitors can't match.
- AI engines answering "agency in Mumbai" pull from: Google Business Profile,
  local directories, and pages that literally say Mumbai. All three are cheap
  to own at city level.

## The 3 fronts

### 1. On-site (done in code)

- `/software-development-company-mumbai` — the local landing page: exact-match
  title, Mumbai clients, services, local FAQ, LocalBusiness schema (address,
  geo, hours), links into services/martech/industries/case-studies.
- Mumbai mentioned in llms.txt and footer-level entity data.
- Mumbai prompt group added to the GEO audit panel, so we measure the local
  share of voice separately from pan-India.

### 2. Google Business Profile (owner action — the single biggest local lever)

1. Create/claim GBP for "StackBinary" at the Kandivali East address,
   category "Software company" (+ "Marketing agency" secondary).
2. Fill everything: services list, photos of the office/team, hours, website →
   link to the Mumbai page (not the homepage — keeps the local signal tight).
3. Collect 10–15 Google reviews from past clients — review count + recency is
   the dominant local ranking factor. Ask every happy client, one per week.
4. Post monthly updates (case study snippets) — freshness signal.

### 3. Local citations & directories (owner action, one-time sweep)

Consistent NAP (name, address, phone) everywhere:
- Priority: Clutch (set Mumbai as location), GoodFirms, G2, Justdial, Sulekha,
  IndiaMART, LinkedIn company page (Mumbai HQ), Crunchbase.
- "Top software development companies in Mumbai" listicles — pitch inclusion;
  these pages are exactly what AI engines cite for city queries.

## Measurement

- The `mumbai` prompt group in buyer-prompts.json tracks local share of voice
  in every audit run — watch it separately; it should move within 4–8 weeks,
  well before pan-India terms.
- Google Search Console: filter queries containing "mumbai" — impressions on
  the new page are the leading indicator.

## Expansion trigger

When Mumbai share of voice holds above ~50% for a month, clone the play for
the next city tier (Pune, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR) — the landing-page template
and audit structure are reusable; only proof points and address change
(remote-first for cities without an office — say so honestly on the page).
