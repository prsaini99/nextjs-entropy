## One paragraph per service

**Upfirst** is the lowest entry price with a full feature set: $24.95 for 30 calls, $59.95 for 90, $159.95 for 300 and $299 for 600, with a 14-day trial and no card. Per-call billing means a 12-minute call costs the same as a 1-minute one, and spam and sub-15-second calls are excluded.

**Dialzara** starts at $29 for 60 minutes and steps to $99 (220 minutes), $199 (500) and $349 (1,000). Overage drops from $0.48 to $0.35 a minute as you move up. Calendar sync is locked to the $99 plan and above, so the $29 tier is a message-taker rather than a booker.

**Rosie** offers 250 minutes for $49, the most generous allowance at the low end, with spam detection on every plan. Direct calendar booking and live transfers arrive on the $149 Scale plan (1,000 minutes); Growth at $299 adds 2,000 minutes. Overage pricing is not published.

**Goodcall** bills by unique caller rather than time: $79 a month per agent covers 100 unique numbers with unlimited minutes, $129 covers 250 and $249 covers 500, at $0.50 per extra caller. A repeat customer who calls ten times counts once, which suits businesses with a loyal base; a lead line where every caller is new pays full rate.

**My AI Front Desk** bundles voice, SMS, chatbot and email into $99 a month with 200 voice minutes, then meters at 25 credits a minute (about $0.25). The $20 Basic plan includes no voice minutes at all.

**Smith.ai AI Receptionist** has a free plan with 25 calls a month and $3 a call after that, a $150 Pro plan spanning 75 to 300 calls at roughly $2 a call, and a $500 Enterprise plan from 300 calls. Smith.ai also runs a human-staffed service from $300 for 30 calls, used below as the human benchmark.

**NextPhone** charges $199 a month flat for unlimited inbound calls with booking, CRM integration, recording and spam filtering included. No meter makes it the easiest bill to forecast and the most expensive option under about 100 calls a month.

**Ringly** starts at $349 for 1,000 minutes (about 500 calls) and $799 for 2,500, built around e-commerce order, return and product calls. It is priced for stores with a real call queue rather than a solo practice.

## What to test before signing

Every service above offers a trial or a free plan. Use it to test the four things that decide whether the bill in the table is the one you get.

**Spam.** Call your own number from a blocked ID and a VoIP number. On per-call and per-minute plans, every robocall the AI picks up is billed unless the vendor excludes it. Upfirst and Smith.ai publish an exclusion policy; most others do not.

**Accents and background noise.** Have three people with different accents call from a car and a kitchen. Transcription quality is the difference between a booked appointment and a vague message.

**Booking depth.** Ask it to book, reschedule, then cancel. Several entry plans send a booking link rather than writing to the calendar; check which plan you are on.

**CRM handoff.** Confirm the call summary lands in your CRM with the caller's number and intent, not in an inbox. Many of these products run on platforms such as Vapi and Retell; [voice AI pricing per minute](/insights/voice-ai-pricing-per-minute-2026) explains what those platforms charge if you want to compare.

## FAQ

### How much does an AI answering service cost for a small business?

Between $24.95 and $349 a month at list price. Per-call plans start at $24.95 for 30 calls (Upfirst), per-minute plans at $29 for 60 minutes (Dialzara), per-caller plans at $79 for 100 unique callers (Goodcall), and flat unlimited plans at $199 (NextPhone).

### What is the difference between AI receptionist pricing per call and per minute?

Per-call pricing charges the same for a 1-minute and a 12-minute call, so it suits long conversations; per-minute pricing suits short calls. At a three-minute average, per-call AI works out at $0.60 to $0.70 a call and per-minute AI at $1.05 to $1.35.

### Is an AI phone answering service cheaper than a human answering service?

Yes, by a factor of 8 to 15 at the same volume. At 100 calls a month, per-call AI costs about $70 against about $915 for a human-staffed service, and at 500 calls about $299 against $3,800.

### Can an AI answering service book appointments?

Yes, but not always on the entry plan. Upfirst, Goodcall, NextPhone and Smith.ai include booking on every plan; Dialzara needs the $99 plan and Rosie the $149 plan for direct calendar writes.
