The short version of HubSpot vs Pipedrive for a sales-led team of ten: Pipedrive Growth costs about $4,700 a year, HubSpot Sales Hub Professional costs about $12,300 in year one, and the gap is mostly the automation, marketing and reporting depth that HubSpot bundles and Pipedrive sells as add-ons. Salesforce Starter Suite undercuts both at $3,000 for ten seats but climbs steeply once you need Pro Suite. Brevo is the cheapest of the four when email volume, not seat count, is the thing you are paying for.

Every price below was checked against the vendors' pricing pages and 2026 third-party guides in September 2026. Prices are USD, annual billing unless stated, and they move, so re-check before signing. ## Where each one wins

**HubSpot** wins on marketing automation and on having one object model across sales, marketing and service. If your team runs sequences, lead scoring, landing pages and a mailing list from the same records, Professional is the tier that does all of that, and the price reflects it. Our earlier piece on [HubSpot alternatives](/insights/hubspot-alternatives-rent-vs-own) covers the marketing contact tiers, which are the other metered line to watch.

**Pipedrive** wins on pipeline simplicity and on price at every seat count in the table. The product is a sales pipeline first, and a team that lives in the deal board will be productive on day one. What it does not include is email marketing: Campaigns is a paid add-on on every tier, including Ultimate, and Premium is the first tier that bundles LeadBooster, Projects and Smart Docs.

**Salesforce** wins at the enterprise end: multi-currency, territory management, revenue forecasting and an admin ecosystem no other vendor here matches. Starter Suite is a credible entry point at $25, but Pro Suite quadruples the seat price and Core doubles it again.

**Brevo** wins when email and SMS volume is the cost driver. Pricing by emails sent rather than contacts stored is why a 200-seat Brevo account lands at $80,000 while the same seat count elsewhere is $165,000 and up. The CRM is a sales layer on a marketing platform, not the reverse.

## The contract traps

**Seat cliffs.** Pipedrive Lite and Growth are cheap per seat, but the features most teams want by month three (automation on Growth, LeadBooster and documents on Premium) sit one tier up. HubSpot's cliff is larger: sequences and workflow automation start at Professional, so the real entry price for a sales team is $90 a seat plus $1,500, not $15 a seat.

**Annual lock.** Salesforce Pro Suite and above are annual only. HubSpot's annual discount is small on Professional ($90 against $100) and larger on Starter. Pipedrive's annual discount is $10 a seat a month on Lite and Growth and $30 on Premium and Ultimate, which is the strongest incentive of the four to commit.

**Add-on stacking.** Brevo's Sales seats plus a marketing plan plus SMS plus a dedicated IP is a different bill from the $31 you first saw. HubSpot's quoting tool is a separate paid add-on. Salesforce's Agentforce for Sales starts at $125 a user a month on top of the edition. Price the stack you will run in month six, not the seat.

**Onboarding as a condition of purchase.** HubSpot's $1,500 and $3,500 fees are mandatory and front-load year one by 12% on a ten-seat Professional account.

## FAQ

### Is Pipedrive enough, or do I need HubSpot?

Pipedrive is enough for a team whose work is moving deals through a pipeline and whose marketing runs elsewhere. HubSpot becomes necessary when the same team needs sequences, lead scoring, landing pages and email marketing on one set of records, and that need is what the $90 Professional seat and $1,500 onboarding pay for.

### What does HubSpot really cost for 10 sales users?

About $12,300 in year one on Sales Hub Professional: ten seats at $90 a month on annual billing, plus the mandatory $1,500 onboarding fee. Starter at $15 a seat is far cheaper but has no sequences or workflow automation, so it rarely holds a sales team past the first few months.

### Is Salesforce cheaper than HubSpot?

At the entry level, yes: Salesforce Starter Suite is $25 a user with no onboarding fee, so ten users cost $3,000 against HubSpot Professional's $12,300. Above that, Pro Suite at $100 and Core at $195 a user are more expensive than HubSpot's $90 and $150 equivalents, and both are annual only.

### Why is Brevo so much cheaper at 200 seats?

Because Brevo prices its marketing platform by emails sent, not by contacts or seats, and its sales seats are a flat $31 add-on. Two hundred seats plus a Professional marketing plan come to about $80,000 a year, against $165,600 on Pipedrive Ultimate and $363,500 on HubSpot Enterprise. The trade is a lighter CRM than the other three.
